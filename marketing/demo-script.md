# Marksmith Demo Scripts

**Target Audience:** Professionals, students, and writers who use AI but need native Word/PPT documents.
**Tone:** Plain, human, factual, understated. No hype.
**Platform:** Windows 11 only.

---

## 6-Second Hook (TikTok / Reels / Shorts Variant)

**Visual:** Fast split-screen. Left: Pasting a ChatGPT markdown response with a KaTeX matrix. Right: The exported Word document opens, zooming in on the same matrix, now a fully native, properly stacked, clickable Word equation.
**On-Screen Text:** AI math → Native Word equation.
**VO:** Stop retyping your AI output. Marksmith turns markdown math and diagrams into native Word shapes, instantly.

## 15-Second Demo Script (Quick Ad / Pre-roll)

**Beat 1: The Math**
* **Visual:** Windows 11 desktop. User pastes AI text with a 3x3 math matrix and piecewise function into Marksmith, clicks "Export to Word".
* **On-Screen Text:** Paste AI output. Get native documents.
* **VO:** Marksmith takes your AI chat output and turns it into real Word documents. 

**Beat 2: The Equation & Diagram**
* **Visual:** The Word doc opens. User clicks the matrix (showing Word's native equation bounds), then clicks a Mermaid flowchart, dragging a box to show it's made of native shapes, not an image.
* **On-Screen Text:** Real equations. Editable shapes.
* **VO:** Math like matrices become native equations. Mermaid diagrams become editable shapes you can actually restyle.

**Beat 3: The Privacy Hook**
* **Visual:** The app works while the Wi-Fi is toggled off in the Windows taskbar. Marksmith logo and "Windows 11" badge appear.
* **On-Screen Text:** 100% Local. Zero cloud.
* **VO:** And it runs entirely on your PC. Your work never leaves your device.

## 45-Second Demo Script (Full Feature Walkthrough)

**Beat 1: The Setup**
* **Visual:** Screen recording of Marksmith on Windows 11. The user pastes a block of ChatGPT markdown containing a 3x3 matrix, a piecewise function, and a Mermaid flowchart into the editor.
* **On-Screen Text:** Paste your AI chat.
* **VO:** You've got your response from your AI assistant, complete with complex math and diagrams. When you paste it into Marksmith, it doesn't just copy the text.

**Beat 2: The Math Reveal**
* **Visual:** Cursor clicks the "Export to Word" button. The Word document opens. The user clicks on the matrix, revealing the native Word equation editor bounds perfectly stacking the rows and columns.
* **On-Screen Text:** Real, editable equations.
* **VO:** It converts the markdown into a real, native Word equation that you can actually edit—even matrices and piecewise functions. No more retyping formulas.

**Beat 3: The Diagram Reveal**
* **Visual:** The user scrolls down to the Mermaid flowchart in the Word document. They click a node in the chart, change its fill color, and drag it slightly to show the connecting line moves with it.
* **On-Screen Text:** Diagrams become native shapes.
* **VO:** And that Mermaid diagram? It's not a pasted picture. It's rebuilt as native Word shapes in the original colors, completely ready to restyle.

**Beat 4: The Formatting & Cleanup**
* **Visual:** Quick cuts showing the document's polished cover page, auto-updating table of contents, and a clean paragraph of text without any AI citation tags (like `[7]`).
* **On-Screen Text:** Cleaned up and branded.
* **VO:** It also strips out tell-tale AI artifacts and applies your own document branding, giving you a clean, professional file.

**Beat 5: The Privacy Promise**
* **Visual:** User clicks "Export to PPTX" while hovering over a "100% Local" indicator in the app. Marksmith logo and "Available for Windows 11" end card.
* **On-Screen Text:** Your data stays yours.
* **VO:** Best of all, Marksmith runs completely on-device. No cloud processing, no accounts. Your work never leaves your PC.
