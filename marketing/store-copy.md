# Marksmith

## Short Description
Turn AI chats into polished, private PDF & Word documents on your local device.

## Long Description
Marksmith is a Windows 11 desktop application that turns AI-chat output from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Copilot into professional, client-ready PDF, Word (DOCX), PowerPoint (PPTX), and EPUB documents. 

AI assistants format text in recognizable ways, leaving behind citation artifacts, pseudo-headings, and excessive formatting. Marksmith detects the source, strips the tell-tale artifacts, and re-themes the content into your own house style. Designed for complete privacy, the app runs 100% on-device and makes zero external network calls. No account, no cloud, works offline.

Marksmith natively rebuilds complex elements that usually break in word processors. Mermaid diagrams are drawn as native, editable Word shapes (not images), and LaTeX/KaTeX math is converted into real, editable Word equations, including matrices and piecewise functions.

Marksmith offers a free tier for PDF exports. Pro features, including DOCX export and automation, are available as a one-time purchase of $39 AUD.

## Features
- **Native Word Diagrams:** Rebuilds all Mermaid diagram types (flowchart, sequence, class, ER, state, C4, block, kanban, packet, sankey, gantt, pie, mindmap, timeline, journey, gitgraph, quadrant, xychart, requirement, architecture) as native, editable Word shapes.
- **Editable Word Equations:** Converts LaTeX/KaTeX math into native, editable Word equations (OMML), including matrices, piecewise functions, arrays, binomials, fractions, integrals, and more.
- **100% Local & Private:** Runs entirely on-device with zero external network calls. Mermaid, KaTeX, and fonts are bundled.
- **AI Formatting Cleanup:** Detects the assistant source and automatically strips tell-tale artifacts to re-theme the document in your style.
- **Professional Branding:** Apply a cover page, logo, document-wide typeface, self-updating table of contents, and repeating table headers.
- **Live Markdown Editor:** A side-by-side live editor that re-renders the document with every keystroke.
- **Automation & Batching:** Folder and clipboard watchers, batch conversion, and a local REST API.
- **Browser Extension:** A "Copy as Markdown" extension to send chats directly to the app.
