# Marksmith Press Kit

## Boilerplate Descriptions

### 25-Word Boilerplate
Marksmith is a Windows 11 desktop app that converts AI-generated text into polished, native PDF and Word documents, completely offline, while removing AI formatting quirks.

### 50-Word Boilerplate
Marksmith is a 100% local Windows 11 application that transforms raw AI-chat output from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude into professional PDF, Word, PowerPoint, and EPUB documents. It detects the source assistant, cleans up recognizable formatting artifacts, and converts Mermaid diagrams and LaTeX math into native, editable Word objects.

### 100-Word Boilerplate
Marksmith is a privacy-first Windows 11 desktop app designed to turn raw AI-chat output—from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Copilot—into polished, client-ready PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and EPUB documents. Instead of just copying and pasting, Marksmith detects the source assistant and strips recognizable formatting artifacts to re-theme the text into your own house style. It operates entirely offline, making zero network calls. Standout features include the ability to convert all Mermaid diagram types into native, editable Word shapes, and transforming LaTeX/KaTeX math—including matrices and piecewise functions—into real, editable Word equations (OMML). 

## Why It Matters
- **Total Privacy:** Everything happens 100% locally on your device with no cloud processing or network calls, keeping sensitive data completely secure.
- **Native Editability:** It doesn't just paste pictures of diagrams and math; it uses a proprietary engine to reconstruct Mermaid diagrams and LaTeX math as native, editable Word shapes and equations.
- **Professional Polish:** Automatically turns raw AI output into a branded, structured document that matches your house style, without looking like it was machine-generated.

## Honesty Caveats
- Marksmith is available for Windows 11 only.
- Sankey diagram flow-ribbon widths and architecture-diagram icon glyphs are not reproduced exactly as they appear in Mermaid, though structure, color, connectivity, and text are maintained.
- "Stripping AI tells" means re-formatting the output to match a human house style; it is not designed to "beat" AI or plagiarism detectors.
- Math conversion handles the vast majority of AI-generated math (including matrices, piecewise functions, sums, integrals, and fractions), but extremely exotic LaTeX may still fall back to standard rendering.

## Feature List
- 100% local, on-device processing with zero network calls.

- Converts LaTeX/KaTeX math (including matrices and piecewise functions) into native, editable Word equations (OMML).
