## The short version

Marksmith is a desktop application. It converts documents **on your device**. We do not run a
service that receives your documents, and we do not want them.

## What we do NOT collect

- **The content of your documents.** Conversion happens locally. Your files are not uploaded to us.
- **Your document file names, folder structure or Document Galaxy contents.** These stay on disk.
- We operate no analytics or telemetry service that receives your usage data. [VERIFY before
